Senior C++ Interview Trap Questions Checklist

This checklist is organized by category and priority (★★★★★ = highest
interview frequency).

1. STL & Containers (★★★★★)

1.  Why is std::vector special?
2.  Difference between size() and capacity().
3.  How does std::vector grow internally?
4.  Why is push_back() amortized O(1)?
5.  When are iterators invalidated?
6.  Why is std::list rarely used?
7.  Is std::set ordered?
8.  Difference: set vs unordered_set.
9.  Difference: map vs unordered_map.
10. Why can unordered_map degrade to O(n)?
11. What is cache locality?
12. reserve() vs resize().
13. push_back() vs emplace_back().

2. Modern C++ Language (★★★★★)

1.  const vs constexpr.
2.  constexpr vs consteval.
3.  virtual vs override.
4.  override vs final.
5.  Why always use override?
6.  What is noexcept?
7.  Copy constructor vs move constructor.
8.  Rule of Three.
9.  Rule of Five.
10. Rule of Zero.
11. nullptr vs NULL vs 0.
12. std::move() really moves?
13. auto and decltype.

3. Object-Oriented Programming (★★★★★)

1.  What is object slicing?
2.  Why should a base class destructor be virtual?
3.  Composition vs inheritance.
4.  Why prefer composition?
5.  Static vs dynamic polymorphism.
6.  What is a vtable?
7.  Cost of virtual functions.
8.  Can constructors be virtual?
9.  Can static functions be virtual?
10. Pure virtual functions.

4.  RAII.

10. Alignment and padding.
11. Memory fragmentation.
